# Code of Conduct

ROM Checker welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Everyone taking part in project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful, useful, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions and reviews.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain disagreements constructively and accept good-faith corrections.
- Share only information you are authorized to disclose. Do not post ROM contents, credentials, private paths, or another person's personal information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Do not use a public issue for a sensitive report. The maintainer will review reports and may remove content or restrict participation when necessary. The project does not currently publish a response-time guarantee or an independent appeals process.

This code of conduct applies to project discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces operated for ROM Checker.
